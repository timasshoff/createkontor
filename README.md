### > 🖋️ | Found and manage a company in a fully simulated economy
Create: Kontor simulates a full economy including market, prices, demand, economic cycles and policy rates.

### > 🏭 | Integrate your Create Mod Factories and Logistics Networks
Use the Create Mod to produce items and connect your Create Logistics Network to automate production.

### > 📊 | Set prices and sell products in fully simulated markets
Calculate cost, observe markets and sell your products at the best price.

### > 🤝 | Hire employees to support you
Employees allow you to grow your company, gather new information and automate processes.
 
### > 📈 | Grow your company and upgrade your legal form
Make money and grow your company to upgrade your legal form and get access to new features!

### > 🖥️ | Make informed decisions
Use the intuitive, powerful GUIs of Create: Kontor to view diagrams, charts and live information from your company.

### > ➕ | And more!
Single- and Multiplayer friendly.
Integration with many mods and other Create Addons.
Fully customizable through data packs and configs.

![How it works](https://github.com/timasshoff/createkontor/blob/main/docs/Subtitle%20How%20It%20Works%20Render.png?raw=true)

Using Create: Kontor you can found a company to **sell your items on a simulated market**. You will need to license your products to receive requests for your licensed products. Accept a request and you will need to produce the requested items. The price you set determines how many requests you will get. By hiring **employees**, you will get access to more features and information about your business!

Create: Kontor simulates **markets** for many different items. Markets have changing prices and changing demand. The economic situation in your world also affects markets: A recession might be harder for your business than an upswing.

See **Getting started** for more info. Or hire a **consultant** in-game and ask him.

<br><br>

![Getting Started](https://github.com/timasshoff/createkontor/blob/main/docs/Subtitle%20Getting%20Started%20Render.png?raw=true)

### > 1️⃣ | Found a company
Craft a **Kontor Desk** and right-click it. Enter a name and decide whether you want to take the founder's loan. After founding, you can right-click the Kontor Desk to access the management UI.

### > 2️⃣ | Hire a lawyer
Craft a **Lawyer's Desk** and sit down a villager next to it. Right-click the villager to open the lawyer UI. Buy a license for your first product(s) in the **License Catalog**. 

### > 3️⃣ | Participate in a market
With your new license, you are able to **participate** in the markets of the bought license. Right-click the Kontor Desk and participate in one market using the **Markets** tab. Set a price and you will start receiving requests for that product.

### > 4️⃣ | Your first request
Once you participate in a market, you will receive requests for that product. View and accept them in the **Requests and Orders** tab of the Kontor Desk. An accepted request will turn into an **order** that you will need to fulfill in time, so start producing the required items to fulfill your first order!

### > 5️⃣ | Delivering your first order
Craft a **Shipping Exit** and place it down next to any inventory (e.g. a chest). When powered, the Shipping Exit will try to fulfill any order it can using items from its adjacent inventories. When an order has been completed, the order will disappear from the Order Book and the payout will be booked to your bank account!

### > 🎉 | Congrats! You just delivered your first order!
What's next? Make money 💸! Grow your company. Upgrade your legal form. Hire new employees. Expand into new markets.

Hire a **consultant** for more info.

### > 🚨 | Updating to new versions
**ALWAYS** create a **backup** before updating Create: Kontor. This mod has a lot of save data. Changing versions might break this save data and therefore your economy / companies. Although I try my best to not break existing worlds with updates, I do not take responsibility for any damage that might happen when updating Create: Kontor. You can use a backup mod like <a href="https://modrinth.com/mod/simple-backups">Simple Backups</a> to automatically create backups of your world.

<br><br>

![Configuration](https://github.com/timasshoff/createkontor/blob/main/docs/Subtitle%20Configuration%20Render.png?raw=true)

### > ⚙️ | Config Files
Most constant values are configurable through the config files. Note that changes are only applied after restarting or leaving / rejoining the world. /reload is not enough. Be careful when changing the default settings and **always make backups**!

### > 📦 | Datapacks
Most of the data that Create: Kontor uses to simulate the economy is configurable / expandable through data packs. This includes:

- Raw Materials
- Markets
- Market Group Definitions
- Licenses
- Legal Forms
- Process Costs
- Bank Loan Offer Types

Be careful when changing datapacks in an existing world. **Always make backups** before changing anything. 

See the wiki for more detailed info on how to create custom datapacks for Create: Kontor.

<details>
<summary>Why is this mod a Create Addon?</summary>
Create: Kontor only really makes sense when you have the ability to build a factory and automate item production. Create is therefore the best fit for Create: Kontor because it offers wide automation capabilities and a full logistics system that is heavily integrated into Create: Kontor. Additionally, Create has a huge addon ecosystem.<br>
The core of Create: Kontor, which simulates the economy, is fully independent of Minecraft and Create. While not planned (!), it would be possible to extract this core and integrate it into other Minecraft mods.
</details>

<details>
<summary>What does "Kontor" mean?</summary>
"Kontor" is an old German word and means something like the office of a businessman. The closest English translation would be "Office", but Create: Office sounds really lame, so I decided to stick with Create: Kontor.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How performant is Create: Kontor?</summary>

After the creation of a new world, Create: Kontor will measure biome frequencies to calculate land prices. This process might take a few minutes and hurt performance a bit, but it's a one-time process. Additionally, the desks of employees load 3x3 chunks around them. I have not observed any other performance issues. If you find any severe performance issues, please create a GitHub Issue <a href="https://github.com/timasshoff/createkontor/issues">here</a>.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Which versions does / will Create: Kontor support?</summary>
Create: Kontor will always support the newest Create Version. Backporting and ports to other modloaders are not planned.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use Create: Kontor in Singleplayer?</summary>
Yes! Create: Kontor is not a mod specifically for SMPs or trading in multiplayer. Instead, it fully simulates an economy and is equally playable in single- and multiplayer.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use Create: Kontor on SMPs?</summary>
Yes, Create: Kontor has full multiplayer support. But depending on the size and expected player number of your SMP, I encourage you to rebalance Create: Kontor for your server using the Config Files and Datapacks. A server with 5 active players will probably need a different balancing to keep the economy stable and playable than a server with 200 active players.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I use Create: Kontor in modpacks?</summary>
Yes, you can! See the wiki on how to configure Create: Kontor and add support for other mods.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Has AI been used in development?</summary>
Developing a mod of this size is hard and time-consuming. I want to be fully transparent and honest about my AI usage. AI can be a powerful tool and accelerate development, but I think it should never be used as anything more than development assistance. 
Here is an overview of my AI usage during development:

**What AI has NOT been used for:**
- Implementing entire features (e.g. using prompts like "Implement feature XY")
- Designing or developing ideas
- Creating art (e.g. textures)
- Generating any other kind of content in the mod (e.g. texts, tooltips, mechanics)

**What AI has been used for:**
- Writing unit tests
- Generating example values for testing
- Generating commands for debugging (initially I wanted to remove these commands again, but I decided to keep them just in case someone needs them)
- Research (technical and content-related research)
- Translation (only singular words / phrases, never using prompts like "Translate this .json file")

All AI-generated code has been marked with a comment saying that the code is AI-generated. If you have any questions or opinions about my AI usage, please let me know!

</details>
