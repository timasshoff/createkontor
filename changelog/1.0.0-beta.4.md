## Beta 4 for v1.0.0

This is a beta: Things will change and worlds might break between versions. Please make frequent backups of your world.
If you have any feedback, suggestions or problems, please let me know!

### Additions
- The Kontor Desk now displays the amount of employees a company has
- Raw Materials now have an optional field `min_legal_level` which defines the minimum legal level a company must have in order to be able to buy this raw material from the buyer.
- Market Export Command `/kontor market export <product> [company]`: Exports all recorded data from one market to a CSV file. If a company name is provided, some company information regarding that market will be exported as well. The file is saved in the world folder under `kontor/export`.

### Changes
- Company Config: Purchase Markup 1.6 >>> 1.8
- Company Config: Auto Purchase Markup 1.9 >>> 2.1
- Products can now only be bought from the Buyer if the company has the required legal level it **would** need to acquire a license for that product.
- Several raw materials adjusted to have a minimum legal level:
  - Raw Copper: Minimum Legal Level 2
  - Raw Iron: Minimum Legal Level 2
  - Raw Zinc: Minimum Legal Level 2
  - Raw Gold: Minimum Legal Level 3
  - Quartz: Minimum Legal Level 2
  - Asurine: Minimum Legal Level 2
  - Crimsite: Minimum Legal Level 2
  - Ochrum: Minimum Legal Level 3
  - Veridium: Minimum Legal Level 2
  - Lapis Lazuli: Minimum Legal Level 3
  - Emerald: Minimum Legal Level 3
  - Diamond: Minimum Legal Level 4
  - Ancient Debris: Minimum Legal Level 4
  - Magma Cream: Minimum Legal Level 3
  - Blaze Rod: Minimum Legal Level 3
  - Ender Pearl: Minimum Legal Level 4
  - Shulker Shell: Minimum Legal Level 4
  - Wither Rose: Minimum Legal Level 3
  - Chorus Fruit: Minimum Legal Level 3
- Netherite Ingot License: Minimum Legal Level 3 >>> 4
- Logistics Manager: Minimum Legal level 1 >>> 3 (this does not really change anything because connecting a logistics network always required at least legal level 3)
- The reference cost of products made with recipes with multiple outputs now distributes cost using a `stable cost` (= the cost of the cheapest recipe that only produces this item). Some reference cost and therefore some prices will change because of this. 

### Fixes
- Fixed prices of certain items dropping to $0.00. The reference cost of a product made with a recipes with multiple output no longer depends on its own market price (this used to make the market price sink every day).
