## Beta 4 for v1.0.0

This is a beta: Things will change and worlds might break between versions. Please make frequent backups of your world.
If you have any feedback, suggestions or problems, please let me know!

### Additions
- The Kontor Desk now displays the amount of employees a company has
- Raw Materials now have an optional field `min_legal_level` which defines the minimum legal level a company must have in order to be able to buy this raw material from the buyer.
