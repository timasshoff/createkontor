## Beta 4 for v1.0.0

This is a beta: Things will change and worlds might break between versions. Please make frequent backups of your world.
If you have any feedback, suggestions or problems, please let me know!

### Additions
- Market Export Command `/kontor market export <product> [company]`: Exports all recorded data from one market to a CSV file. If a company name is provided, some company information regarding that market will be exported as well.
