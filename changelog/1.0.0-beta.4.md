## Beta 4 for v1.0.0

This is a beta: Things will change and worlds might break between versions. Please make frequent backups of your world.
If you have any feedback, suggestions or problems, please let me know!

### Additions
- Market Export Command `/kontor market export <product> [company]`: Exports all recorded data from one market to a CSV file. If a company name is provided, some company information regarding that market will be exported as well. The file is saved in the world folder under `kontor/export`.

- X

### Changes
- The reference cost of products made with recipes with multiple outputs now distributes cost using a `stable cost` (= the cost of the cheapest recipe that only produces this item). Some reference cost and therefore some prices will change because of this. 

### Fixes
- Fixed prices of certain items dropping to $0.00. The reference cost of a product made with a recipes with multiple output no longer depends on its own market price (this used to make the market price sink every day).
